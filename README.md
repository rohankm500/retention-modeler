I built this after taking Exam P in an attempt to familiarize myself with work that would be expected of me when I get a job. I started with a simple question, "If an insurer increases premiums, how much more money would they make" 
To test it out I had an AI assistant generate synthetic data for me where I controlled exactly how customers react to price increases (younger customers and past claimants are more likely to leave, long-tenure customers are stickier)
With this data I saw how revenue peaks at an 8.5% increase but underwriting profit climbs to about 23% as the customers that leave tend to be the riskier ones anyway.
I took this as the biggest lesson of this project and now my next step is to compare this to an actual insurance dataset.
